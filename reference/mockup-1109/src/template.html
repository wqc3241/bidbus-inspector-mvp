Internal MVP 1109

Inspector app MVP: build notes

The inspector finds the car in the listing leads, checks the seller's documents and the Carfax history, inspects the car in one camera pass, reviews it, gets the seller's signature and sends the record. Bidbus makes the condition report from it.

01 The flow

Search the listing leads→Car brief: the seller's documents as they upload→Check ID at the car: the person against the documents, owner question→One camera pass: Exterior (windshield VIN, 360 video), Interior (engine first start), Walk-around (paint, tires, trunk, hood, roof), OBDII, Mechanicals, Underbody; Look for on every shot; questions at the end of each section→Damages: confirm or label every damage photo→Review: one page per section→Summary: VIN, mileage, overall condition, note→Disclosure and seller signature→Upload; Bidbus makes the condition report

The inspection takes about 18 minutes.

05 The inspection

  • Questions: the MVP's own list, data/question-list.json: . Answers keep its ids.
  • Car type per item: all, gas (gas, diesel, hybrid, plug-in hybrid), EV, plug-in (EV and plug-in hybrid), hybrid. The fuel type comes from the VIN decode and is never asked.
  • Every check starts as OK (road test Not done, Keys Two or more work); one problem per check. Levels: Disclosed, Yellow, Red, Not listed until fixed, Ends the visit, Not checked.
  • Readings come from the photos (AI) and the inspector confirms them: VIN, mileage, tire brand, size and tread, paint mils (8 or more is High and ticks Panel repainted), EV battery health, number of keys.
  • Tool screens (paint meter, OBDII, EV screens, mechanicals and underbody photos) take a photo or an upload; the source is kept per photo. OBDII other images, EV screens and catalytic converters take several photos.
  • Structure is a damage type on Exterior body panels and on Interior carpet, headliner, dash, door panels, cargo and Other; it answers C8 Structure and frame. Exterior body panels also offer the five Paint and panels problems, stored as Paint at that panel.
  • Photo category is the question's section in the question list (Start, Exterior, Paint, Tires, Underbody, Interior, Mechanicals, OBDII, Additionals), not the camera pass.

06 Visit time and location

  • Start: time and GPS at Start inspection. End: time and GPS at the signature, or when the visit is ended.
  • Shown on the Summary and the finished car, with the distance from the seller's address.
  • Recorded with no signal too.

07 Seller signature

  • The seller sees the findings in plain words, the disclosure, their name (filled from the owner, editable) and a signature pad under "I have seen the inspection findings for this car."
  • Disclosure: the inspection records what our inspector could see and test at the visit, without lifting the car or taking it apart; it describes the car at that time and is not a warranty; Bidbus is not responsible for problems that appear later or could not be seen or tested; the seller has told us every problem they know of. Sign stays off until "I have read and agree to this disclosure" is ticked.
  • Saved: signature, name, disclosure version, time and GPS.
  • Findings only: nothing about dealer protection, guarantees or buybacks.

08 Upload and the condition report

  • One record per car when the seller signs: answers, photos, videos and 360 frames, readings, damages with the AI first guess, signature, start and end time and place. Built by inspectionPayload; examples in samples/inspection-*.json.
  • Media upload one file at a time and resume after a lost signal.
  • Bidbus checks the record against data/issue-catalog.json and makes the condition report (tools/cr_from_inspection.py, examples in samples/cr-*.json): issues in the app's words, the highlight (structural damage, keys, odor, mechanical), CR rows with their photos and damages, tires, paint, videos, 360 view and the seller signature. It also fills the seller self-inspect fields the listing uses.
  • Seller and dealer reports: the 360 view leads the Exterior photos, then eight exterior views from its frames (left front 1, left side 5, left rear 10, rear 14, right rear 19, right side 23, right front 28, front 32).
  • Damage dots on the 360 view: each confirmed Exterior, Paint or Tires damage with a photo gets a dot on its part, on the frames that show that part; the dot turns with the car and hides on the far side. Amber, red for a Red or worse damage. One dot per part (a wheel and its tire are one part), with the number of damages when there are several. Tapping a dot opens a card with each damage's photo and line (it scrolls); tapping a damage opens its photo full screen; closing goes back to the 360 view. The timeline under the view has a mark per dot that turns the car to it. Mirror, headlight, tail light and fuel door use the side the inspector picked, else the side the AI reads from the damage photo, else no dot; Other and Other lights get none. The part positions are worked out on the server after upload; until then the view has no dots.

09 Offline

  • The leads and briefs download with signal; list, search and briefs work without it. The full Carfax report needs signal.
  • Everything is saved on the phone as it is taken; closing the app or losing signal loses nothing.
  • An upload link sent with no signal goes out when signal returns.
  • After the signature the car shows Saved on phone, then Uploading, then Inspected · report ready.
  • A small bar on every screen shows when there is no signal.

10 Not in this MVP

  • Price, listing and auction screens; booking and dispatch; driver and pickup work.
  • Ravin AI damage detection on the walk-around.
  • Editing the question list in the app.

11 Done when

  1. An inspector finds a car by search, reads its brief and finishes an inspection typing only engine codes, corrections and the note.
  2. A car with no problems needs no taps on checks.
  3. Start stays off until both documents are in and the owner question is answered.
  4. One camera pass covers every section; the only stops are the end-of-section questions.
  5. The walk-around becomes a 360 view; eight of its frames are the exterior photos.
  6. Every damage photo has a place, type and severity, from the AI guess or the inspector.
  7. Show the seller stays off until every section is reviewed and every required answer is in.
  8. The seller cannot sign without ticking the disclosure.
  9. An inspection done fully in airplane mode uploads in full when signal returns.
  10. The condition report appears in the admin after upload; documents never reach a dealer.
